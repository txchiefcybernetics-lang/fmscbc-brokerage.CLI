# About FM Sardan Customs Brokerage and Consultancy (FMSCBC)

**OFFICIAL NAME**

FM Sardan Customs Brokerage and Consultancy (FMSCBC)

## Overview

FM Sardan Customs Brokerage and Consultancy (FMSCBC) is a full‑service customs brokerage and trade advisory firm specializing in import and export clearance, tariff classification, customs valuation, regulatory compliance, and trade facilitation. We deliver tailored solutions that reduce clearance times, control costs, and mitigate customs-related risk for importers, exporters, freight forwarders, and logistics partners.

## Vision

FM Sardan Customs Brokerage and Consultancy envisions to be a globally engaged trade partner creating sustainable impact in society and the economy.

## Mission

FM Sardan Customs Brokerage and Consultancy seeks to provide strategic customs brokerage, trade facilitation, and consultancy services through the highest standards of compliance, integrity, professionalism, and innovation, committed to advancing responsible trade and sustainable economic development.

## Core Values

- Compliance: Accurate, lawful processing of trade documentation.
- Integrity: Honest advice and transparent billing.
- Professionalism: High standards in delivery and client communication.
- Innovation: Continuous improvement and practical use of technology to streamline trade.
- Sustainability: Supporting responsible trade practices that benefit society and the economy.

## Services

- Customs brokerage and clearance (imports & exports)
- Tariff classification and HS code advisory
- Customs valuation and duty optimization
- Import/export documentation and filing (manifests, commercial invoices, certificates)
- Trade compliance programs & audits
- Entry and post‑entry amendments
- Tariff preference and origin documentation (FTA rules of origin)
- Consultancy for regulatory licensing and permits
- Support for customs appeals and penalty mitigation
